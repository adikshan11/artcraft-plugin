How it works

Just ask.

Your assistant opens the right Craft app, does the work and saves the result to your private library. Every edit makes a new file, so your original is never touched.

The apps are Storytold's own, run as they are. BridgeCraft is the bridge between them and your chat.

About

An extension for the ArtCraft apps.

ArtCraft brings Storytold's open-source Craft apps together. BridgeCraft extends those same apps into ChatGPT, Claude and other AI assistants.

BridgeCraft is community-built and run by Adithya Shankaran. It is not an official Storytold or ArtCraft product. I have proposed that Storytold adopt it; follow along in RFC-0016.
