01 / What it is

An extension for the ArtCraft apps.

BridgeCraft extends Storytold's open-source Craft apps, the ones ArtCraft brings together, into ChatGPT, Claude and other AI assistants. They are the same apps, run as they are: you ask in a chat, and your assistant uses them for you.

Status BridgeCraft is community-built and run by Adithya Shankaran. It is not an official Storytold or ArtCraft product. I have proposed that Storytold adopt it; follow along in RFC-0016.

03 / How it works

Just ask.

Your assistant picks the right tool, runs it on your file and hands back the result. Every edit makes a new file, so your original is never touched.

  • Video, image and PDF edits, with e-signatures
  • {{ hosted_apps|length }} Craft apps for documents, decks, design and sound
  • One private library for each account

04 / Built-in tools

And the everyday edits.

Built-in tools for the jobs that come up every day.

01

Video and audio

Trim, join, resize, caption and convert videos, add or extract audio, and grab still frames.

02

Images

Crop, resize, adjust colour, add text and borders, and layer one image over another.

03

PDFs and e-signatures

Read, reorder, merge, fill in, watermark and sign PDFs. A signature comes with an audit page.
